CAE / DATA-DRIVEN DESIGN · RESEARCH DEMONSTRATOR

Can an ML warm start reduce topology-optimization cost?

Plane-stress Q4 FEM · SIMP · density filter · optimality criteria · supervised ridge baseline

Scope: small 16×8 mesh, one cantilever family, 24 load positions by default, fixed volume and material. This is not an industrial validation or a reproduction of the 2019 U-Net results. A speedup below 1 means slower. Failed cases are retained.

Experiment contract

Entire FEM cases and their mirror-equivalent partners are grouped before splitting. Ridge regularization is selected on validation compliance only. Five early updates, inference, volume projection, FEM checking and refinement are all included in hybrid timing. The reference is a converged local OC solution, not a certified global optimum.

Quality gate: both runs converged, compliance ≤105% of reference, volume error <10⁻⁶, free-DOF residual <10⁻⁷. Pixel connectivity is a separate diagnostic. Physical density fields are displayed without thresholding.

Raw evidence: test_results.csv · manifest.json · dataset.npz

Measured outcome

6/6 quality gates passed, from 3 independent symmetry groups. Worst direct prediction: 1.244× reference compliance. Refinement is essential for the weakest predictions.

What this does not establish

No new geometry family, 3D, nonlinear material, contact, fatigue, manufacturing constraint, binary reanalysis or commercial-solver correlation is included. Timing is single-run CPU on a dense reference backend. Dataset generation/training is offline and its amortization must be considered before deployment. A failed quality gate requires investigation or a full reference solve; fallback timing has not been measured.
