# Research development roadmap

This historical planning document is retained for continuity of numerical development.
The active research directions are documented in [the project README](../README.md),
[the numerical development protocol](development-v2.md), and
[the scientific ML research plan](scientific-ml-roadmap.md).

## Ongoing engineering objectives

- Reproducible finite-element solutions with clear assumptions.
- Independent equilibrium, energy and sensitivity verification.
- Controlled data generation and honest train/validation/test separation.
- Classical optimizers alongside trainable surrogate and operator models.
- End-to-end runtime, equilibrium, compliance and failure accounting.
- Mesh convergence, external solver correlation and improved problem diversity.
- Documented methods, accessible code and opportunities for collaboration.

This document records the evolution of the research questions. It does not
represent the completion of numerical verification or industrial qualification.
