Decision

6/6 thin-membrane runs pass the predeclared gates. At the smallest thickness, compliance differs by 0.17%–0.27%; displacement L2 by at most 0.29%; reaction L2 by at most 0.24%.

3/3 unit-thickness diagnostic runs fail. The largest compliance discrepancy is 8.1%. Failures are retained rather than hidden or accommodated by changing the thresholds.

This is cross-solver verification, not experimental validation. It supports the linear membrane implementation on three cases at one mesh and load position. It does not qualify strength, nonlinear response, manufacturing geometry, or all ML predictions.

Why thickness matters

CalculiX expands CPS4 into C3D8 internally. The reference solver directly uses a 2D plane-stress constitutive matrix. For the thickness study, ECCX=Ereference/t so E·t, Poisson ratio, in-plane mesh, forces and in-plane constraints are preserved. This is membrane equivalence, not an assertion that the three-dimensional models are identical.

Reducing thickness from 0.01 to 0.001 changes the resulting compliance by less than 0.002% in these cases. A small residual formulation discrepancy remains; it is not rounded away.

Unit force is a linear-response normalization. Physical force levels would need scaling to satisfy small-strain assumptions for a physical material and geometry.

Acceptance criteria fixed before the comparison

Relative compliance ≤1%; global displacement relative L2 ≤1%; constrained in-plane reaction relative L2 ≤5%; absolute force-resultant imbalance ≤10⁻⁵ for a unit applied force. These are project-specific demonstrator criteria, not industrial standards. CalculiX printed output has finite precision.
